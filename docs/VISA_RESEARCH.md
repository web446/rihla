# Visa-free destinations for Lebanese passport holders (research, November 2026)

Rihla's idea: trips from Beirut to countries a **Lebanese passport** can enter **without arranging a
visa in advance**. This note records the research behind the 6 destinations in `seed.py`.

> Visa rules and flight routes change often. Wikipedia and travel sites are secondary sources: the site
> tells travelers to confirm with the embassy or an official source before travelling.

## The 6 destinations on the site

| Country | Entry (Lebanese passport) | Max stay | Direct from Beirut | Popularity with Lebanese |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Turkey | Visa-free | 90 days in any 180 | Yes: Istanbul (IST, SAW), Ankara, Antalya, Adana; Bodrum/Dalaman in summer | 5/5: ~245,000 Lebanese visitors in 2025 (TÜİK via the Turkish embassy); Istanbul was Beirut's busiest route in 2024 (~594,000 passengers) |
| Armenia | Visa on arrival or e-Visa | 120 days per year | Yes: Yerevan, MEA, 2-3 a week | 4/5: in "almost all" Lebanese agencies' packages (Armenian official, 2025) |
| Egypt | Visa-free **only** on charter arrivals at Sharm El Sheikh, Hurghada or Borg El Arab, tour groups of 10+, or under 16 / over 50 | 90 days in any 180 | Yes: Cairo (EgyptAir, MEA) | 4/5 (estimate) |
| Jordan | Visa-free (iris scan; not for emergency/temporary passports) | 3 months in any 6 | Yes: Amman (Royal Jordanian, MEA) | 3/5 (estimate) |
| Georgia | Visa-free; travel health insurance mandatory (≥ 30,000 GEL) | 1 year | Uncertain: Georgian Airways stopped in April 2026; MEA reported for summer 2026 | 3/5 (estimate) |
| Malaysia | Visa-free (fingerprints; Digital Arrival Card) | 90 days in any 180 | No: via Doha, Dubai or Istanbul | 3/5 (estimate) |

Only Turkey has published visitor numbers; the other popularity levels are estimates based on direct
flights, travel-agency packages, communities and pilgrimage traffic.

## Removed from the first version (visa required for Lebanese)

Italy and Greece (Schengen visa), Japan (visa), Thailand (e-Visa required in advance).

## Other countries with no visa needed in advance (not on the site)

- **Visa-free:** Iraq (30 days, ID card accepted), Syria (180 days), Iran (15 days, announced May 2026),
  Oman (14 days with hotel, insurance and return ticket), Ecuador, Barbados, Rwanda, Haiti, Suriname,
  Dominica, Micronesia, Tajikistan (conditional), Northern Cyprus, Macau, Phu Quoc (Vietnam).
- **Visa on arrival:** Maldives (30 days, free), Mauritius (60), Nepal (90), Madagascar (90),
  Cambodia (30), Ethiopia (90, Addis Ababa only), Sri Lanka (30, or ETA), Djibouti, Mozambique,
  Timor-Leste, Comoros, Guinea-Bissau, Palau, Samoa, Tuvalu, Marshall Islands.
- **Electronic authorization:** Kenya (eTA, 90 days, USD 32.50), Seychelles (3 months, EUR 10).
- **Careful:** the Republic of Cyprus (Larnaca) requires a visa; only the north is visa-free.
